Privacy

Plain English, short. Last updated 11 November 2026.

No account, no tracking

You don't sign up. Your browser makes up a random device ID the first time you visit, and your boards are filed under it. There are no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

What we store

  • Your lists: the text you type, the tasks made from it, and what you do with them (order, times, done, deleted). Old boards are kept when you start a new one, until you delete your data.
  • Rate-limit counters: how many requests came from your device ID and your IP address in the last few minutes. They expire within about a day.
  • In your browser: your device ID and a couple of settings (radar zoom, whether you've seen the examples).

The database is run by Neon in the United States (AWS, Ohio). The app runs on Vercel, which keeps short-lived request logs that include IP addresses.

Who else sees what

  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to receive the text you type into the big box, so they can sort it into a list. Tasks you add one by one are not sent to them. We currently use free models; free model providers may log prompts or use them to improve their models. Don't type anything you wouldn't want a third party to see.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • adsb.lol supplies the aircraft on the radar. We fetch that public data from our server; nothing about you is sent to them.

Delete my data

This removes every board and counter stored for this device, then gives this browser a fresh device ID. It can't be undone.

Text already sent to OpenRouter can't be recalled. Vercel's request logs and the database's short-term restore history expire on their own.
