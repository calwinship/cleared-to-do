        HOW IT WORKS

        • Tell the tower what's on your plate, messy is fine. It sorts everything into a list. Or add tasks one by one.
        • Sequence is what you're doing now, in order. Holding is not today. Landed is done.
        • Drag a task by its ⋮⋮ handle to reorder it, or drop it into Holding or back into the Sequence.
        • Tap a task to rename it, change its time, or delete it. Tap ✓ when it's done.
        • The radar shows your list as traffic: numbered blips on the approach and in the hold. Tap one to jump to it. Real aircraft around Dublin drift by in the background.
