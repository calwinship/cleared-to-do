        HOW IT WORKS

        • You're the controller at Dublin Airport. Your tasks are the arrivals.
        • Tell the tower what's on your plate, messy is fine. It sorts everything into a sequence. Or add tasks one by one.
        • Sequence is what you're doing now, in order. Holding is not today. Landed is done.
        • Start the top task. On the radar it leaves its hold so it touches down when its time is up. Not done by then? It goes around and keeps circling until you tick it off or add time.
        • Drag a task by its ⋮⋮ handle to reorder it, or drop it into Holding. Tap a task to rename it, change its time, or delete it. Tap ✓ when it's done.
        • Focus mode keeps it calm: a big timer, your list, a small radar. Fun mode adds live Dublin traffic, arrivals in your list, and tower-style messages.
